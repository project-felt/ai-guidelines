## Complete all necessary internal reviews

> **Red Hat internal resources**
>
> Some links on this page are for Red Hat employees only. Sign in with your Red Hat account to open them.

Red Hat teams developing AI-enabled features must follow any applicable internal review requirements. These may include an AI Assessment (AIA), a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA), or reviews by other teams. Following this public design guidance does not replace those reviews.

An AIA is Red Hat's internal review of a new or changed AI use case. For details on when an assessment is required and how to submit one, see [AI Assessments (AIA) guidance ](https://url.corp.redhat.com/ai-assessments). (Red Hat employees only · Sign-in required)

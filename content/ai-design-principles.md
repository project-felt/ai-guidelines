Regardless of the application or components, when designing for an AI-enabled experience:

- **Be transparent**: Make it clear to users that they're interacting with AI using labels and visual cues.
- **Make it personable, but not human**: AI from Red Hat should be polite and follow our voice and tone, but it shouldn't act as though the user is interacting with a human.
- **Stay within the Red Hat brand and design language**: Follow the [brand standards](https://brand.redhat.com) and [PatternFly](https://patternfly.org) and [design system](https://ux.redhat.com) standards so that AI-enabled experiences look and feel like Red Hat.
